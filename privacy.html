Privacy policy

For Aria, a private household assistant operated privately by one household. Effective 6 September 2026.

Who this covers

Aria is used by the members of one household, on devices the operator has personally enrolled. There are no other users. If you are reading this and are not a member of that household, Aria holds no data about you.

What Google data Aria accesses

Aria signs in to Google using a dedicated Google account that belongs to the household and owns no data of its own. Household members have shared their calendars and shopping lists with that account, in the same way they share them with each other. Through it, Aria can:

  • read, create, change and delete events on the calendars shared with it (Google Calendar, scope calendar.events);
  • read, add, remove and clear items on the shopping lists shared with it (Google Keep).

Aria does not access email, contacts, Drive files, photos, location, or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Only to carry out what a household member asks by voice: for example, adding milk to a shopping list, or moving a dinner on the calendar. Aria reads a calendar or list when asked, changes it when asked, and tells the person what she did.

Where the data goes

Requests are processed on a single computer inside the home. Spoken audio is transcribed on that computer and is not sent to Google. The text of a request, and the results of any calendar or list action, are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to decide what to do; Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy. Google data is not sold, shared with any other third party, or used for advertising, analytics, or training.

What is stored, and for how long

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored on the home computer, readable only by the account that runs Aria.
  • A small log of each request (the words heard, the action taken, and timings) is kept on the same computer for troubleshooting, and is deleted when no longer needed.
  • Aria keeps a short list of which shopping list each item usually goes to, so she does not have to ask twice.

Calendar events and list items themselves live in Google, under the household members' own accounts, and are not copied elsewhere.

Your control

Any household member can remove Aria's access at any time by un-sharing their calendar or lists with the household account, or by revoking the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The operator can remove a device from Aria by deleting one line of configuration.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Aria's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
