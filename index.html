Aria

A private household assistant, run privately by one household.

Aria is a personal voice assistant that runs on a computer in our home. Members of the household talk to her from their phones to manage a shared calendar and shopping lists. She is not a product, is not offered to the public, and has no users outside the household.

This site exists because Google requires an application that uses Google Calendar and Google Keep on a person's behalf to publish a home page, a privacy policy and terms of service. Those pages describe exactly what Aria does with the data she can reach.

What she can do

Read and change events on the household calendars she has been given access to. Read, add to, remove from and clear the household's shopping lists. Set reminders. Nothing else: no email, no contacts, no files, no web.

Where things run

Speech recognition, the assistant's voice, and all of her logic run on a single machine in the house. The only outside services involved are Google, for the calendar and lists, and Anthropic's Claude, which provides the language model that turns speech into actions.
